1. What is your understanding of DevOps and Cloud Engineering?

   DevOps is a collaboration of software development and operations. It is a practice or process that aims for improving        the application delivery, by ensuring that there is a proper automation with a maintained code or application quality and    also ensuring that there is continuous testing and continuous monitoring in place.

   Cloud Engineering is the practice of designing, building, implementing and managing cloud based systems and
   infrastructure to improve business efficiency, scalability and security.
